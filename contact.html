What Happens After You Contact Us

A clear, transparent path from first message to project kickoff.

1

Initial Response

We'll acknowledge your message within 24 hours and may ask a few clarifying questions to better understand your needs.

2

Discovery Call

We'll schedule a brief call (usually 30-45 minutes) to discuss your project, goals, challenges, and how we might help. This is a two-way conversation to ensure we're a good fit.

Once approved, we'll kick off the project with a detailed planning session, establish communication channels, and set clear milestones. Then we start building.
